# Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard — Final Capstone

## Project summary
Analyze Superstore retail sales to understand revenue, profitability, regional and category performance, and potential business improvements.

## Dataset scope
- Records: 9,994
- Orders: 5,009
- Customers: 793
- Date range: 2014-01-03 to 2017-12-30

## Main findings
- Total sales: $2,297,200.86
- Total profit: $286,397.02
- Profit margin: 12.47%
- West region leads sales and profit.
- Technology leads category sales and profit.
- Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies have the weakest aggregate profit.
- 2017-11 was the highest-sales month.
